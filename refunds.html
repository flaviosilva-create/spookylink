Refund Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

SpookyLink wants customers to be comfortable trying its digital products. This policy applies to SpookyLink Invitation, Haunted Mystery, and the bundle.

30-day refund request window

You may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds are handled through Paddle's buyer-support and transaction procedures. Visit Paddle Buyer Support and identify the SpookyLink purchase. Paddle may request order details to locate the transaction.

What happens after a refund

When a refund is approved, access to the paid digital feature may be revoked. Because invitation artwork is generated and downloaded in your browser, we cannot remotely delete an image already saved on your device.

Legal rights and payment disputes

This policy does not restrict any mandatory refund, withdrawal, or other consumer rights that apply in your country. If a local law or Paddle's applicable policy grants a greater right, that right takes precedence. For questions about a transaction, begin with Paddle Buyer Support or see our Contact & Support page.

SpookyLink purchases are one-time digital purchases, not subscriptions.
