Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains how SpookyLink handles information when you use our independent digital invitation and browser game website.

1. Information entered in the invitation editor

The event title, host, date, time, location, and message you type into the invitation editor are processed in your browser to render your preview and create your download. This version does not send those invitation fields to a SpookyLink server or use a SpookyLink user account. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in invitation fields.

2. Purchases and payment information

Checkout is handled by Paddle, which acts as Merchant of Record for transactions. Paddle and its payment partners process information required to complete and support a purchase, such as contact, billing, transaction, fraud-prevention, and tax information. SpookyLink does not receive or store your complete payment card details. See Paddle's Privacy Policy for its own processing practices.

3. Browser storage

After a completed checkout, this site stores a local browser marker to unlock the relevant download or game. This local marker is stored on your device and is not a cloud account or server-side purchase record. You can remove it by clearing site storage in your browser, but doing so may remove the local unlock marker. Do not use this as a secure archive of your purchase receipt.

4. Service providers and technical data

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It loads Paddle.js to provide checkout, Google Fonts for typography, and html2canvas from a public CDN to export invitation images. These providers may receive technical information such as IP address, browser information, and request logs when your browser connects to their services. Their own terms and privacy policies govern their processing.

5. Why information is used

Information is used to display the website, generate the invitation you request, open and complete checkout, provide purchase support, prevent misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations. We do not sell the invitation text you enter in the local editor.

6. Retention, security, and children

Invitation editor data remains in your browser unless you copy or share it yourself. Purchase records needed for payment, tax, or support are handled by Paddle and its systems. No website can guarantee absolute security. SpookyLink is a general event-creation tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices and requests

You can avoid entering personal details, close the page, clear browser storage, and contact support with questions about privacy. For checkout data, you may also need to contact Paddle. The operator must provide real support contact details on the Contact & Support page before the website is submitted for payment-provider review.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the service changes. The latest version and date will be published on this page.
