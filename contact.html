Contact & Support

Support for SpookyLink digital purchases and the website.

Orders, receipts, payments, and refunds

For a purchase processed by Paddle, use Paddle Buyer Support. Have your order receipt available so the support team can locate the transaction.

Website and technical issues

For site bugs or technical issues, use the SpookyLink GitHub issue tracker. Do not post payment details, order receipts, addresses, or other sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Privacy questions

For information processed by Paddle during checkout, review Paddle's Privacy Policy and contact Paddle through its buyer-support channels. Invitation details are processed in your browser and embedded in any invitation URL you choose to share; SpookyLink does not upload invitation fields to a SpookyLink server.

SpookyLink is an independent digital project. The website currently uses Paddle for order support and GitHub for public technical issue reporting. Please do not include sensitive information in public posts.
